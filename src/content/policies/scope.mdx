## Scope of Services

University Navigator, Inc. provides the following services, as included in the
client's selected engagement: academic planning and educational strategy; course
selection guidance; college admissions planning; extracurricular activity planning;
letters of recommendation planning; personal statement and admissions essay review (as
included in the selected package); scheduled Zoom consultation sessions (as included in
the selected package); and educational consulting relating to the student's academic and
college admissions planning.

## Scope Limitations

Services are limited to those expressly included within the selected engagement. Any
service not expressly listed is outside the scope of the engagement.

## Entire Agreement Clause

The service descriptions and policies published on this site constitute the entire
agreement between University Navigator, Inc. and the client, superseding any prior oral
or written representations.

## Educational Disclaimer

> Admission to any college or university cannot be guaranteed. University Navigator,
> Inc. provides professional educational consulting designed to improve a student's
> preparation and application strategy. Final admissions decisions are made solely by
> each institution.

## Independent Research

Clients are responsible for conducting their own independent research regarding colleges
and universities. UNI does not perform independent university research except as a
separately quoted special project.

## Services Not Included

The following are not included in any engagement: independent university research;
transfer-credit evaluations; articulation agreement analysis; contacting colleges or
universities; contacting admissions offices; meetings with schools; legal matters;
school disciplinary matters; appeals; preparation of customized reports; scholarship
searches; financial aid counseling (unless separately offered); visa or immigration
matters; translation services.

## Special Projects

The following are quoted individually as special projects: school disciplinary matters;
academic appeals; university research; transfer strategy; educational pathway
evaluations; customized reports; international educational consulting.

## Family Participation Clause

Parents and guardians are welcome and encouraged to participate in scheduled sessions.
Communications regarding a minor student are conducted with the parent or guardian's
knowledge.

## Change in Scope

Requests for services beyond the selected engagement require a written change in scope
and may be quoted separately.

## Communications Policy

All service communications, including essay submissions, are conducted by email to
[info@universitynavigator.org](mailto:info@universitynavigator.org). Essay reviews are
returned within 72 hours of submission and payment.
