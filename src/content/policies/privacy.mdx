## Information we collect

We collect the information you provide through our contact, inquiry, booking, and
checkout forms: names, contact details, academic background, and — where you choose to
provide it — birthdate and address. Many respondents are minors; we collect only what is
needed to respond to your inquiry and deliver services.

## How we use it

To respond to inquiries, prepare for consultations, deliver contracted services, and
process payments. Marketing email is sent only with your explicit, separate opt-in and
can be withdrawn at any time.

## What we don't do

We do not sell or share your information. Form submissions are transmitted to us by
email and are not persisted with third-party form services. Submission data is excluded
from server logs.

## Information stored in your browser

If you begin the Request for Information form, your answers are saved in your own
browser's session storage so that moving between steps — or reloading the page — does
not wipe what you have entered. This data stays on your device, is never transmitted
until you submit the form, and is cleared when you close the browser tab.

## Service providers

We use a small number of third-party providers to operate this site. Each receives only
the information needed to perform its function:

- **Stripe** — payment processing. Receives billing details and email address for
  purchases. We never see or store full card numbers.
- **Cal.com** — scheduling for free consultations. Receives the booking details you
  enter, including student name, parent or guardian name, current school, graduation
  year, and what you would like to discuss. Because consultations are frequently booked
  for minors, Cal.com processes information about minors as a subprocessor on our behalf.
- **Resend** — delivery of transactional email, including form submissions sent to our
  office and the confirmation emails sent to you.
- **Cloudflare** — website hosting and bot protection (Turnstile) on our forms.
  Turnstile is privacy-preserving and does not track users across sites.

We use no third-party analytics and set no advertising or tracking cookies.
