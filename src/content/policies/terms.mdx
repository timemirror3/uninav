## Services

University Navigator, Inc. provides professional educational consulting services as
described in the selected engagement. The [Scope of Services](/policies/scope) and
[Refund Policy](/policies/refunds) are incorporated into these terms.

## No guarantee of outcomes

Admission to any college or university cannot be guaranteed. Final admissions decisions
are made solely by each institution.

## Payment

Payments are processed by Stripe. The University Counseling Bundle renews every three
months until cancelled. One-time services are billed at purchase; the 72-hour essay
turnaround begins at submission and payment.

## Cancellation

You may cancel the University Counseling Bundle at any time before its next renewal
through the Stripe customer portal linked in your receipt and confirmation emails, or by
emailing [info@universitynavigator.org](mailto:info@universitynavigator.org). Cancelling
stops future renewals; amounts already paid are handled under the
[Refund Policy](/policies/refunds).

## Confidentiality

Student essays and family information are held in full confidentiality and used only to
deliver the contracted services.
